04

Built to be questioned.

The thresholds, test, and exact inputs behind this decision. Every number in this report comes from your paired evaluation data.

Decision rule & statistical method
Minimum support20paired cases per group
Drop tolerance5 ppcandidate minus baseline
Significance0.05Holm-adjusted p threshold

The gate: an evaluation group fails when its pass rate drops by at least the configured tolerance, its support reaches the minimum, and its Holm-adjusted p value is at or below the significance threshold. The overall group and all supported declared slice groups are checked. Unsupported groups never fail the gate.

The test: a one-sided exact paired McNemar test uses discordant cases to test for regression: losses exceeding wins. Holm's step-down correction is applied across the tested overall and declared slice groups. These groups may overlap. Adjusted significance describes evidence of change; it does not explain the cause.

RegressionSupported drop at or beyond tolerance, with adjusted significance. Fails the gate.
WatchSupported negative change that does not meet the regression rule. Review the cases.
ImprovedSupported positive change. A descriptive label, not a significance claim.
StableSupported zero change in paired pass rate.
InsufficientFewer paired cases than the configured minimum. The group stays visible but is not tested.
